Company story

Built by dispatchers, for shippers

NorthLane was founded in 2011 by two former dispatchers who were tired of answering the question "where's my truck?" with a phone call. They bolted GPS units into three leased tractors, built a tracking page over a weekend and promised customers they would never have to ask again.

That obsession with visibility shaped everything that followed — from the control tower that re-plans our fleet every 15 minutes to the open APIs that let customers see their freight inside their own systems.
